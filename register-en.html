Greetings

Hello,

First of all, thank you for your interest in NARACHA Taekwondo and for reaching out about registration.

At NARACHA Taekwondo, our training is built on the experience of martial-arts physical conditioning and the fundamental techniques of Taekwondo. Our goal is to help each student grow step by step until they are able to defend themselves.

Of course, the training methods may vary depending on the student's age and individual needs.

Please take a look at the materials below — we hope you find them helpful.

Standard plan: Twice a week (Basic Training)

Please look through the class schedule, decide which time slot you would like to train in, and let us know. Thank you very much.

Make-up Class Policy

Based on two-month registration · The months below (Jul–Aug, etc.) are examples — the same rule applies to every registration period.

① Jul–Aug registration period

For every regular session you miss, you earn a make-up session.

e.g. Attended 14 of the standard 16 sessions
→ 2 make-up sessions earned

② Use them in Sep–Oct

When you attend, your regular sessions are counted first → then your make-ups are used.

e.g. Standard 16 + 2 make-ups
= you can train up to 18 sessions

※ Make-ups you do not use during this period expire at the next registration.

③ Make-ups for Nov–Dec?

New make-ups are earned only for the Sep–Oct regular sessions you missed.

Make-ups from the previous period (Jul–Aug) do not carry over (no accumulation).

Our Educational Philosophy

NARACHA Taekwondo's principled education helps students grow into people this society truly needs —
building right thinking, a generous heart, and a healthy body.

1
A dojang with age-based classes and a belt-level curriculum.
2
A dojang that raises the quality of education through the use of respectful language and strictly separated classes by level.
3
A dojang with a caring class atmosphere, where students never hold back praise and encouragement for one another.
4
A dojang of character education, where students build physical strength through physical activity and learn what it means to be a good person.
5
A dojang taught by professional instructors from top university Taekwondo programs and demonstration teams.

"A true dojang that practices physical training and character education through Taekwondo."

8

Our Promises with Our Students

  • NARACHA students remember the training rules and do their best to keep them.
  • When entering the dojang, students wear their uniform neatly and bow with respect, treating the dojang as a place of self-discipline where body and mind are trained.
  • During training, students wear their uniform and belt properly as a matter of principle.
  • Students always show courtesy and respect to their instructors and fellow students.
  • During training, students focus on the instructor's words and follow them well.
  • Students take part in training with an active, sincere attitude.
  • During training, students focus on effort and improvement, and value the spirit of challenge.
  • After training, students put their equipment back neatly in its place.
  • Students must protect themselves well, and must never misuse the techniques they have learned.

Taekwondo is a martial art of character cultivation,
through which we work to overcome our own shortcomings.

The Symbol of NARACHA Taekwondo

Emblem

The emblem depicts a kick — the most iconic movement of Taekwondo — shaped by combining the Yin and Yang of the Taegeuk. It expresses the dynamic spirit and integrity of NARACHA.

Characters

The penguin — a bird that cannot fly — carries a special meaning: through Taekwondo training, students gain the wings of confidence to realize their dreams and ideals.
